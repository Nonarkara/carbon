# CFO Lite public workflow review · 2 October 2026

Source: https://cfolite.thaicfcalculator.com/ and [TGO's official listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=th.or.tgo.cfolite).

## Observed in the public web UI

- Organization creation / join-by-link prompts, with authentication required.
- History with search, year filter and not-offset / offset status.
- Email/password, Google and ThaiID login options.

No account was created; no calculation, offset transaction or certificate request was submitted. Calculation screens behind login were not inspected. This is a workflow review, not an audit of calculation correctness, security or assurance.

## Published by TGO, not independently demonstrated

The official app listing describes guided Scope 1–3 organizational calculations, scope selection, saved assessments, reports, certificates and Thai/English support. The claim belongs to the publisher.

## Adaptation for Kabonna

The organization-first continuity is useful: organization → reporting period → activity records → footprint → history → offset evidence. The forest workbench solves a different task. The new Bible teaches that distinction, rather than letting province stock masquerade as organizational emissions.

Potential future organizational module: versioned activity/factor register, evidence beside each source, explicit missing-data review, reproducible period history and retirement evidence linked to claims. These are proposals. We have not built a complete CFO inventory, connected TGO factor/registration APIs, or obtained authority to issue official certificates.

The native bilingual Bible implements the immediately useful part: definitions, scope boundaries, factor selection, worked arithmetic, evidence sources, forest calculations, uncertainty and credit gates. It is independent of CFO Lite's branding and code.
